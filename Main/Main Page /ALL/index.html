Ideas worth keeping.

Books I’ve read and ideas I’m still thinking about.

ATOMIC
HABITS

Atomic Habits

James Clear

Small actions, repeated consistently, can lead to meaningful change.

Key ideas
  • Aim to get 1% better each day; small gains compound.
  • Habits run on a loop: cue, craving, response, reward.
  • Focus on who you want to become, not only the result.
THE
PSYCHOLOGY
OF MONEY

The Psychology of Money

Morgan Housel

How people think and behave can matter as much as what they know about money.

Key ideas
  • Behaviour matters more than knowledge.
  • Luck and risk shape outcomes more than we admit.
  • Knowing when you have enough is a skill.
